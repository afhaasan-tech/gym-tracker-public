# Brand name check (provisional) — VORA / ڤورا

**Not legal clearance.** The owner made the final naming decision (VORA | ڤورا), replacing the earlier working name HAYL / حيل. Only limited public web searches were done (2026-11-11). No trademark register (Kuwait, GCC, WIPO, EUIPO, USPTO), domain registrar, app-store or commercial-registry search was performed. Do not treat the name as legally available until a trademark attorney / official search confirms it.

## Findings
- **VORA / ڤورا:** a web search for "Vora" with fitness / sports / gym / Kuwait / Gulf terms returned no app, gym or company with that name in Kuwait or the Gulf (results were other Kuwaiti apps such as Floey, Vigor, STAX, Inspire Pure Fitness). "Vora" is a short word that is very likely used by unrelated businesses elsewhere (other industries and countries); that was not investigated, and the Arabic spelling ڤورا was not searched in trademark registers.
- **Earlier candidates (not adopted):** HAYL / حيل (no exact conflict found; similar names *Hayal Fitness App*, *Hayakom*; "حيل" is also a common Arabic word), هيلا / Heila and همّة / Himma (common words, likely crowded), Shadda (rejected without clearance).
